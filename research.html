      Honors and awards

      Gastrointestinal Endoscopy · April 2025

      Editor's Choice and featured on the journal cover

      Our meta-analysis comparing endoscopic submucosal tunneling with flexible septotomy for Zenker's diverticulum was selected as Editor's Choice and listed first among the featured articles on the April 2025 cover of GIE, the official journal of the American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy.

      Delgado LM*, Meine GC*, Santo P, Magalhães CM, Pimenta de Figueiredo VL, Ramos JA, Arantes VN. Gastrointest Endosc. 2025;101(4):751-761.e30. doi:10.1016/j.gie.2024.11.043

      The same study also won 2nd place among free-topic oral presentations at SBAD 2024.

      Fort Lauderdale, FL, USA · 2026

      Best Abstract, Head and Neck Session, Society of Robotic Surgery

      Best abstract of the head and neck session at the SRS 2026 Annual Meeting for our transoral endoscopic thyroidectomy vestibular approach versus open thyroidectomy meta-analysis in Graves' disease. Co-author.

      Athens, Greece · 2026

      Research groups

      Aug 2026 to present · Belo Horizonte, Brazil

      UFMG Department of Surgery

      Undergraduate Research Fellow

      I collaborate with Leandro de Aquino at the UFMG Department of Surgery on a prospective cohort measuring breast sensitivity before and after surgery, to be published as Breast Sensitivity After Mastopexy with Implants Using the L Short-Scar Technique: A Prospective Cohort Study. The project's focus is sensory outcomes after aesthetic breast surgery.

      Jan 2024 to present · São Paulo, Brazil

      Surgery Research Group

      Research Collaborator

      I collaborate with the Surgery Research Group, led by Bernardo Pompeu with the general surgery residents of Hospital Heliópolis, designing and running systematic reviews and meta-analyses and publishing them in journals such as JAMA Surgery, Colorectal Disease and Hernia. The group's topics of interest are colorectal and anorectal surgery, hernia repair, hepatobiliary, bariatric and robotic surgery.

      45 publications · 16 as first author

      Jun 2025 to present

      Thoracic Surgery Research Group

      Research Collaborator

      I collaborate with the Thoracic Surgery Research Group, led by Rachid Oliveira, designing and running systematic reviews and meta-analyses, including survival analyses from reconstructed individual patient data, and publishing them in journals such as Annals of Surgical Oncology, Journal of Thoracic Disease and Lung Cancer. The group's topics of interest are pleural mesothelioma, lung transplantation and ECMO, minimally invasive lung resection and chest wall surgery.

      15 publications · 4 as first author
